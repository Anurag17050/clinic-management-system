Key Design Notes

  • Circular FK resolution: DEPARTMENT.head_doctor_id → DOCTOR and DOCTOR.dept_id → DEPARTMENT creates a circular dependency. Resolved by inserting DEPARTMENT with head_doctor_id = NULL, inserting doctors, then updating. In Oracle, the FK is declared DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED.
  • PRES_MEDICATION composite PK: (rx_id, med_id) is a natural key — no surrogate needed since the combination is inherently unique and meaningful.
  • No double-booking: UNIQUE(doctor_id, appt_date, time_slot) enforced at schema level; backed by a trigger in Phase 2 for richer error messages.
  • One-to-one relationships: PRESCRIPTION, MEDICAL_RECORD, and BILLING each enforce appt_id UNIQUE — exactly one record per appointment.
